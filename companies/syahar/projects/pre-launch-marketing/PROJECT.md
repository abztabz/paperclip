All marketing planning and execution documents for Syahar's pre-launch phase, collated as project artifacts. Owned by the Marketing Lead; produced by the marketing pod (Content & Community, Creative & Copy, Growth & Outreach). Supports the **Phase 1 — Validate** acquisition workstream (SYA-4, SYA-6, SYA-14, SYA-17, SYA-18).

> **⭐ Golden Rule:** every document here must sell **care and peace of mind** — Syahar is a caregiver platform, never a remittance/money-transfer service. Remittance brands are partners, not competitors.
