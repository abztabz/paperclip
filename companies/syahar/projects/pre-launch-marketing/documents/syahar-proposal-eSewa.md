# Partnership Proposal — Syahar × eSewa

**Date:** 15 June 2026  
**From:** Syahar — Nepal's Caregiver Platform

**Contact:** Partnerships / Business Development Team, eSewa

---

## Who We Are

**Syahar** is Nepal's first dedicated caregiver-matching platform, connecting diaspora Nepali families abroad with vetted, professional caregivers for elderly parents and dependent family members in Nepal.

We serve Nepali communities in the **UK, Gulf (UAE, Qatar, Kuwait), Australia, and South Korea** — families who send remittances home every month and carry the worry of ageing parents living alone.

**Our mission:** Give diaspora families peace of mind by placing a trusted caregiver in the home of their loved ones in Nepal.

---

## The Partnership Opportunity

eSewa is Nepal's pioneer digital wallet and one of the country's most trusted fintech brands. With millions of active users and a growing international remittance presence, eSewa reaches diaspora Nepalis at the exact moment they think about family back home.

We are proposing a **revenue-sharing referral partnership**: eSewa surfaces Syahar to your users; Syahar handles all caregiver matching and placement; and we pay eSewa **NPR 500 per first completed caregiver placement** originating via your referral — with zero operational burden on eSewa.

---

## How It Works

| Step | Who | Action |
|------|-----|--------|
| 1 | eSewa | Place a Syahar link or banner (post-remittance screen, "Family Services" section in the app, or eSewa newsletter) |
| 2 | User | Clicks through to Syahar's eSewa-branded referral landing page |
| 3 | Syahar | Matches and places a vetted caregiver with the family in Nepal |
| 4 | Syahar | Pays **NPR 500 per first placement** from eSewa's referral code |

Only a link placement is required from eSewa — no engineering integration for the pilot.

---

## Why This Is a Natural Fit for eSewa

- **Nepal's most trusted digital brand:** eSewa's trust endorsement of Syahar will drive immediate credibility with families.
- **Diaspora remittance flow:** eSewa's international remittance users are diaspora Nepalis — the core Syahar audience.
- **Super-app ecosystem:** eSewa already offers insurance, loans, and utilities. Adding caregiver services completes the "family care" dimension of the eSewa ecosystem.
- **Emotional alignment:** Sending money home triggers family care emotions. Syahar is the natural next step.
- **Pure revenue upside:** NPR 500 per placement, no ops cost, no minimum commitment.
