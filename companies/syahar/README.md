# स्याहार · Syahar

**Cross-border caregiver platform for in-patient families in Nepal.**

Syahar connects diaspora families working abroad with vetted professional caregivers for their hospitalised relatives in Nepal. Nepali hospital culture expects a family attendant for every in-patient — but labour out-migration has put the earning generation abroad while the ageing generation stays home. Syahar productises the attendant role into a managed, transparent, payable service: families abroad pay for peace of mind, caregivers earn dignified stable income, and Syahar earns a blended ~22–28% take of gross care spend.

This is an [agent company](https://agentcompanies.io/specification) package: a CEO and a five-person founding team that executes the Phase 1 "Validate" launch and grows along a five-year plan toward national leadership.

## How the company operates

**Hub-and-spoke.** The CEO holds the strategy and the phase gates and dispatches work to the founding team. Work flows from the CEO's phase plan into the **Phase 1 — Validate** project as assigned, specialty-matched tasks. The team executes in parallel where dependencies allow:

1. **Legal foundation** (Counsel) → entity, hospital MoUs, insurance, policies, PSP contract — unblocks everything.
2. **Caregiver supply** (Recruiter/QA) → vet, train, and bench caregivers. With Legal, gates the first live placements.
3. **Product MVP** (Lead Engineer) → coordinator console, family app, payments, security.
4. **Operations** (Care Coordinator) → SOPs + design-partner placements; the service is run by hand first.
5. **Acquisition** (Marketing) → open diaspora corridors and feed leads to the Coordinator.

The governing discipline is **phase gates**: capital and ambition advance only when real data clears the prior phase's exit criteria.

### Roles in brief
- **CEO** — owns the plan, capital, and phase gates; assigns work and tracks the Phase 1 exit gate weekly.
- **Legal & Compliance Counsel** — entity, MoUs, insurance, ToS/Privacy, caregiver contracts, licensed PSP; the front of the dependency chain.
- **Caregiver Recruiter / QA Officer** — recruits, vets, trains, benches, and quality-assures caregivers; owns dignity-led retention.
- **Lead Product Engineer** — builds the manual-first MVP (console, family app, payments, security) around the Coordinator's real workflow.
- **Care Coordinator** — runs every placement end-to-end and hand-holds the first design-partner families.
- **Marketing Lead** — opens diaspora corridors (hospital on-site, community, referrals, testimonials) and feeds leads to the Coordinator; coordinates the marketing pod below.
- **Content & Community Agent** — runs the content calendar and diaspora community engagement (replaces freelance content labor).
- **Creative & Copy Agent** — brand voice, landing-page/ad copy, and video scripts (replaces freelance copy/creative).
- **Growth & Outreach Agent** — NRNA/press/remittance partnerships, referral mechanics, and funnel analytics (replaces outreach + analyst labor).
