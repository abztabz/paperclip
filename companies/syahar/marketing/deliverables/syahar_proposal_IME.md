## About Syahar

Syahar is Nepal's first cross-border caregiver placement platform, connecting in-patient families in Nepal with verified, trained caregivers. We serve the Nepali diaspora — families abroad whose elderly parents or recovering relatives need professional in-home care in Kathmandu and beyond.

---

## Why IME + Syahar

IME Remit is Nepal's largest remittance corridor operator, with reach across Gulf, Malaysia, Japan, Korea, and Europe. The families sending money through IME are exactly the families Syahar serves: working abroad, unable to be present, and responsible for aging or ill relatives at home.

A Syahar integration transforms IME from a money-transfer service into a **complete family care platform** — the only operator offering both financial remittance and physical care remittance in a single flow.

## Proposed Partnership

### Model: Referral Revenue Share

| Metric | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Referral fee | **NPR 500 per first confirmed placement** originating via IME Remit |
| Attribution | Unique IME referral link / promo code tracked in Syahar's system |
| Payment cycle | Monthly, within 15 days of month close |
| Minimum payout | NPR 5,000 (waived first 3 months) |

### What We Ask From IME Remit

- Post-transaction prompt in IME app/web: *"Your family received NPR X. Make sure they're cared for — book a verified caregiver from Syahar."*
- Co-branded landing page (Syahar provides design assets)
- Optional: joint press release and social media at launch

### What Syahar Provides

- Full referral tracking dashboard for IME's partnership team
- Co-branded materials in Nepali, English, and Malay/Arabic (for key IME corridors)
- Monthly placement and revenue reports
- Dedicated family support for IME-referred customers
- Partner logo and featured placement on Syahar's website

## IME-Specific Value Points

- Diaspora families from Gulf/Malaysia have the highest caregiver demand (elderly parents, post-surgical recovery)
