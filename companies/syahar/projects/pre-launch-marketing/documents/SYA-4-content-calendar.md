What specific concerns can we address? 🙏

> Yes. We never share individual family details publicly. Care reports go only to the designated family contact you specify. Our privacy policy is at syahar.com/privacy.

### Moderation Rules (Facebook Groups)

1. **Value-first, always.** Never post "Join us!" without providing something useful — a tip, a story, an answer to a real question.
2. **No spam in comments.** Don't drop the waitlist link in comments unless someone directly asks or the context makes it natural.
3. **Respond within 24 hours** to all DMs and tagged comments.
4. **Flag for human review:** medical questions, family in crisis, media inquiries, hostile comments requiring nuanced response.
5. **Engagement bait is off.** No "Like if you agree!" posts. Ask genuine questions.
6. **Language:** Default bilingual (NE/EN). For Gulf corridor, consider Arabic alongside Nepali for Gulf-Nepali worker posts.
