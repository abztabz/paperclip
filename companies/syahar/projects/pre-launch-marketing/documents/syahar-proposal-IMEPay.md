# Partnership Proposal — Syahar × IME Pay

**Date:** 15 June 2026  
**From:** Syahar — Nepal's Caregiver Platform  
**To:** IME Pay (IME Digital Solution Ltd.)  
**Contact:** Business Development Team, IME Pay

---

## Who We Are

**Syahar** is Nepal's first dedicated caregiver-matching platform, connecting diaspora Nepali families abroad with vetted, professional caregivers for elderly parents and dependent family members in Nepal.

We serve Nepali communities in the **UK, Gulf (UAE, Qatar, Kuwait), Australia, and South Korea** — families who send remittances home every month and carry the worry of ageing parents living alone.

**Our mission:** Give diaspora families peace of mind by placing a trusted caregiver in the home of their loved ones in Nepal.

---

## The Partnership Opportunity

IME Pay is one of Nepal's most trusted remittance and digital payment brands, with a large and loyal user base of diaspora Nepalis sending money home. Your users are our users.

Every time a diaspora family sends money through IME Pay, they are thinking about the people they left behind. Syahar turns that financial act of love into a complete care solution.

We are proposing a **revenue-sharing referral partnership**: IME Pay surfaces Syahar to your diaspora users; Syahar handles all caregiver matching and placement; and we pay IME Pay **NPR 500 per first completed placement** originating via your referral.

---

## How It Works

| Step | Who | Action |
|------|-----|--------|
| 1 | IME Pay | Place a Syahar banner/link in the IME Pay app (e.g. post-remittance success screen, "Care for Family" tab, or monthly newsletter) |
| 2 | User | Clicks through to Syahar's IME-branded referral landing page |
| 3 | Syahar | Matches and places a vetted caregiver with the family in Nepal |
| 4 | Syahar | Pays **NPR 500 per first placement** originating from IME Pay's referral code |

Implementation requires only a referral link with IME Pay's unique partner code — no API integration needed for the pilot.

---

## Why This Is a Natural Fit for IME Pay

- **Shared audience:** IME Pay's remittance users are diaspora Nepalis — Syahar's primary segment.
- **Post-transaction moment:** After completing a remittance transfer, users are emotionally primed to think about family welfare. A Syahar prompt is natural, not intrusive.
- **Trust transfer:** IME Pay is already trusted by millions of Nepalis. Endorsing Syahar extends that trust to a new category of family care.
- **Revenue with no ops cost:** IME Pay earns NPR 500 per placement with zero customer support or fulfilment burden.
