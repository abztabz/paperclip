# Partnership Proposal — Syahar × Khalti

**Date:** 15 June 2026  
**From:** Syahar — Nepal's Caregiver Platform  
**To:** Khalti (Khalti Digital Wallet, FinTech Pvt. Ltd.)  
**Contact:** Business Development / Partnerships Team, Khalti

---

## Who We Are

**Syahar** is Nepal's first dedicated caregiver-matching platform, connecting diaspora Nepali families abroad with vetted, professional caregivers for elderly parents and dependent family members in Nepal.

We serve Nepali communities in the **UK, Gulf (UAE, Qatar, Kuwait), Australia, and South Korea** — families who send remittances home every month and carry the worry of ageing parents living alone.

**Our mission:** Give diaspora families peace of mind by placing a trusted caregiver in the home of their loved ones in Nepal.

---

## The Partnership Opportunity

Khalti has built one of Nepal's most vibrant digital ecosystems — a super-app where Nepalis pay bills, shop, send money, and access services. Your diaspora-facing remittance and international payment corridors put you in direct contact with our core audience.

We are proposing a **revenue-sharing referral partnership**: Khalti surfaces Syahar within its platform or communications; Syahar handles all caregiver matching and placement; and we pay Khalti **NPR 500 per first completed placement** originating via your referral.

This is pure upside for Khalti — a new revenue stream with zero operational cost and a value-add for your users.

---

## How It Works

| Step | Who | Action |
|------|-----|--------|
| 1 | Khalti | Add a Syahar referral link or banner (in-app "Family Care" section, post-payment success screen, or user newsletter) |
| 2 | User | Clicks through to Syahar's Khalti-branded referral landing page |
| 3 | Syahar | Matches and places a vetted caregiver with the family in Nepal |
| 4 | Syahar | Pays **NPR 500 per first placement** originating from Khalti's referral code |

All we need from Khalti is a link placement — no API integration, no engineering work required for the pilot.

---

## Why This Is a Natural Fit for Khalti

- **Super-app synergy:** Khalti already offers a range of lifestyle and utility services. Caregiver matching is a natural extension — "send money home + arrange a caregiver" in one ecosystem.
- **Diaspora remittance users:** Nepalis abroad using Khalti for international transfers are Syahar's primary target segment.
- **Emotional context:** The moment a user sends money to a parent is when they most think about whether that parent is safe and cared for.
- **Brand values:** Khalti's mission to improve Nepali lives aligns with Syahar's care-first positioning.
- **Revenue with zero ops:** Khalti earns NPR 500 per placement with no customer support burden.
