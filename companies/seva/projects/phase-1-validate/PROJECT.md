**Months 1–12 (Year 1) · Budget: NPR 1.25 crore (~USD 9.4K) · Target: 150 cumulative placements**

Objective: prove that diaspora families will pay for managed in-patient care, and that caregivers will deliver to standard, in Kathmandu Valley only. Earn trust before automating.

Phase 1 footprint: 1 city (Kathmandu), 3–5 hospital partners, ~40 caregivers recruited.

## Workstreams & sequencing
1. **Legal foundation** (Legal & Compliance Counsel) — entity, MoUs, insurance, policies, PSP. Front of the dependency chain; unblocks everything.
2. **Caregiver supply** (Recruiter/QA Officer) — vet, train, and bench caregivers. With Legal, gates the first live placements.
3. **Product MVP** (Lead Product Engineer) — coordinator console, family app, payments, security.
4. **Operations** (Care Coordinator) — SOPs and design-partner placements; the product is run by hand first.
5. **Acquisition** (Marketing Lead) — open diaspora corridors and feed leads to the Coordinator.

Independent branches run in parallel; live placements depend on the entity + insurance + ≥1 MoU + a vetted/trained caregiver pool.

## Phase 1 Exit Gate
- 150 cumulative placements
- ≥4.5/5 family satisfaction
- ≥70% caregiver retention
- Contribution-positive per placement
- ≥1 diaspora corridor generating organic referrals

Capital and ambition advance to Phase 2 only when this gate is cleared with real data.
