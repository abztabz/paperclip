## 1. Why this matters now

Syahar's Phase 1 exit gate requires **≥1 diaspora corridor generating organic referrals** and contribution-positive placements. Both are far cheaper to hit if awareness and trust are built *before* we ask anyone to pay. Pre-launch is also when we manufacture our single most valuable asset — **proof** (waitlist demand, design-partner stories, testimonials) — which every later channel compounds on.

We compete on the **cross-border trust layer**, not novelty. Informal attendants *and* Nepal home-care providers (MedEx, Nursing Care Nepal, etc.) already exist — but none offers a UK-diaspora-first, app-mediated experience with vetting, daily reports, transparent home-currency billing, hospital MoUs, and UK-side concierge. That gap is our wedge, and the window is open but not infinite (one provider already markets to NRN families; an app entrant exists). Pre-launch awareness is mostly a **trust-manufacturing** exercise, executed with urgency.

## 2. Market grounding (the research)

The structural demand is unusually large and concentrated, which makes targeted pre-launch reach feasible on a small budget:

- **~3.5M Nepalis work abroad (~14% of the population)**; over 2M+ live abroad longer-term. Earners abroad, ageing parents at home — Syahar's exact wedge. ([IOM/Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepalese_diaspora))
- **Remittances ≈ USD 11.25B in FY2024/25, >25% of GDP** — the money pipe home already exists and flows monthly; Syahar redirects a slice of undirected remittance into accountable care. ([Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20250708/d37b185b6c26428f949e967043038817/c.html), [NRB](https://www.nrb.org.np/contents/uploads/2025/07/Current-Macroeconomic-and-Financial-Situation-English-Based-on-Eleven-Months-data-of-2024.25-1.pdf))
- **The "paying" diaspora is geographically concentrated** (higher disposable income, smartphone-native, guilt-sensitive):
  - **USA ≈ 264,000** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepalese_diaspora))
  - **Australia ≈ 213,000 Nepal-born**, 5× growth in a decade, AUD/USD ~467M/yr remitted ([nepalnews](https://english.nepalnews.com/s/explainers/everything-you-need-to-know-about-nepali-diaspora-in-australia-and-their-links-to-nepal/))
  - **UK ≈ 80–90,000** (England 77,349 + Scotland/Wales/NI) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepalese_in_the_United_Kingdom))
  - **Gulf: Qatar ≈ 267k, Saudi ≈ 298k, UAE rising**; **Malaysia 500k–700k**; **South Korea ≈ 90k** — huge but lower-income, mixed willingness-to-pay. ([Nepal Labour Migration Report 2024](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/398610144_Nepal_Labour_Migration_Report_2024))
- **A ready-made distribution graph exists: the NRNA** (Non-Resident Nepali Association) — National Coordination Councils in **90 countries**, with Australia alone running **8 state councils + women's, youth and student forums** and 22,500+ Facebook followers. This is the single highest-leverage pre-launch partnership channel. ([NRNA](https://nrna.org/), [NRNA Australia](https://www.facebook.com/NRNAAustralia/), [NRNA Europe](https://nrna.eu/))

**Implication for targeting (revised per Reports #1–#2 + CEO focus override):** **UK only** for now. *Within the UK*, lead with the **work-/study-migrant cohort whose parents are in Nepal** — concentrated in **London (Hounslow/Hillingdon/Ealing/Woolwich) and Reading/Basingstoke** — and use **Rushmoor/Gurkha networks for trust halo and testimonials**, not as the primary demand pool. Australia/USA/Gulf are paused. Win the UK corridor's trust first; lead with trust, not price.

## 4. Channel plan (pre-launch, costed)

| **Landing page + waitlist** | Single page: promise, how-it-works, NPR-transparent pricing teaser, **"Reserve a caregiver for your parent" waitlist** + WhatsApp opt-in. Built by Lead Engineer (SYA-2) | NPR 0 (in-MVP) + NPR 10,000 domain/analytics | The conversion engine; segments leads by corridor |
| **Targeted paid social (test)** | Small UK/AU lookalike + interest (NRNA, Nepali community, remittance apps) → waitlist. Optimise, don't scale yet | NPR 60,000 test budget (~NPR 5,000 CAC) | ~12 qualified signups + channel learning |
| **Hospital pre-wiring** | Warm the 3 MoU hospitals' desks/ward nurses with materials *before* launch so referrals flow on day one (ties to SYA-6) | NPR 30,000 print | Day-one inbound, lowest CAC (~NPR 800) |

**Discipline:** every channel drives to **one place — the waitlist** — tagged by corridor so we can prove "≥1 corridor with organic pull" before spending on placement acquisition.

## 5. Content pillars

1. **The empty chair** — emotional truth: the hospital attendant culture + the parent alone. (Awareness)
2. **Radical transparency** — daily care reports, NPR pricing, vetted caregivers, money-back on first placement. (Trust)
3. **Dignity for caregivers** — the women who deliver care; this differentiates and earns goodwill. (Brand)
4. **Proof** — waitlist counts, design-partner stories, testimonials as they land. (Conversion)

## 6. Pre-launch timeline (T-10 → T-0)

- **T-10 to T-8:** Finalise positioning + messaging; build landing/waitlist (with SYA-2); draft NRNA brief; shoot founder "why" video.
- **T-8 to T-6:** NRNA UK + Australia outreach; open waitlist; begin organic posting in UK groups; recruit 2 micro-ambassadors per corridor.
- **T-6 to T-4:** Host first NRNA webinar; publish 3-video set; launch small paid-social test (UK/AU); start design-partner recruiting (feeds SYA-3).
- **T-4 to T-2:** Pre-wire hospital desks (SYA-6); secure 1 diaspora-press story; convert webinar attendees to waitlist; first testimonials from design partners.
- **T-2 to T-0:** Waitlist → launch-list segmentation; "we're live in Kathmandu" announcement to NRNA + groups + waitlist; referral programme (SYA-14) primed.

## 9. Risks & mitigations

- **Low diaspora trust / slow adoption** → manual concierge, real reports, money-back guarantee on first placement, founder-led video. (Extend pre-launch rather than force.)
- **Spreading thin across corridors** → win UK first; Australia second; treat Gulf/Korea as word-of-mouth seeds only.
- **Awareness without a product to point to** → gate the public "we're live" push to the moment the MVP (SYA-2) + insurance (SYA-7) are ready; until then, drive to waitlist only.
- **Channel dependency on NRNA** → diversify with independent groups + paid test so we're not single-threaded.
