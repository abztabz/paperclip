The active research focus: verified-data market intelligence and analyst modelling for the **UK corridor**, refreshed on a recurring cadence (~every 3 hours / on demand). Each cycle closes data gaps, re-runs the TAM/SAM/SOM and unit-economics model, and produces a numbered report.

> **Methodology:** verified secondary data only (UK Census/ONS, World Bank remittance data, Nepal care-cost pricing) plus a transparent analyst model — **not** primary polling. Modelled figures state their assumptions. A ready-to-field survey instrument lives in the report's §8 for primary validation.
>
> **⭐ Golden Rule:** Syahar is a caregiver platform, never a remittance/money-transfer service.

## Reports
- `documents/uk-market-report-01.md` — **Report #1** (2026-06-16): demographics, UK→Nepal remittance cost, Nepal eldercare pricing; TAM/SAM/SOM (~14,600 households → ~70 UK Phase-1 placements); UK-corridor financials; **key insight — re-aim UK spend at London/Reading work-study migrants (parents in Nepal), not Gurkha-heavy Rushmoor (elders often relocated to UK).**

*Subsequent reports (uk-market-report-NN.md) are appended here each refresh cycle.*

## Open validation items (carried between reports)
- "% of UK Nepali households with a parent in Nepal" and "annual acute-need rate" — the two assumptions that swing the model most (survey §8).
- UK→Nepal bilateral remittance volume & sender count (WB matrix only to 2021).
- Direct-competitor scan (Nepal eldercare services marketing to UK diaspora).
- UK Nepali age-pyramid & income bands (ONS small-population tables).
