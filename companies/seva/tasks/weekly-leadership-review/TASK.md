Every week, review Seva's progress against the **Phase 1 exit gate** and unblock the team:

- **Cumulative placements** (target 150) and run-rate toward it.
- **Family satisfaction** (target ≥4.5/5) and first-report NPS.
- **Caregiver retention** (target ≥70%) and supply-bench health.
- **Per-placement contribution** (must be positive).
- **Diaspora corridors** — is ≥1 producing organic referrals? CAC by corridor.
- **Budget burn** vs the NPR 1.25cr Phase 1 envelope; runway vs the pre-seed raise.
- **Blockers** across Legal, Supply, Product, Ops, and Marketing — assign an owner and next action to each.

Do not advance capital or ambition toward Phase 2 until real data clears every exit criterion. Capture decisions and the week's priorities in a comment the whole team can read.
