सेवा · **Seva** productises the family-attendant role that Nepali hospital culture requires for every in-patient. Nepal's labour out-migration has separated the earning generation (abroad) from the ageing generation (home); Seva fills that absence with a managed, transparent, payable care service. Families abroad pay for peace of mind, caregivers earn dignified stable income, and Seva earns a blended take of ~22–28% of gross care spend.

## How this company operates — hub-and-spoke

The **CEO** holds the strategy and the phase gates, and dispatches work to a five-person founding team. Each function reports directly to the CEO during Phase 1 (manual-first, trust-led):

- **Legal & Compliance Counsel** stands up the entity, hospital MoUs, insurance, policies, and the licensed payments partner contract — the legal foundation every placement depends on.
- **Caregiver Recruiter / QA Officer** builds and certifies the caregiver supply: vetting, health screening, onboarding training, and the on-call backup bench.
- **Lead Product Engineer** builds the MVP platform: coordinator console, family app, payments integration, and the security posture for patient data.
- **Care Coordinator** runs every placement end-to-end — matching, handover, daily reporting, escalation — and hand-holds the first design-partner families.
- **Marketing Lead** opens the diaspora corridors: hospital on-site presence, community outreach, referrals, and trust assets.

Work flows from the CEO's phase plan into the **Phase 1 — Validate** project as assigned tasks. The team executes in parallel where dependencies allow; the legal foundation and caregiver supply gate the first live placements, which gate the marketing scale-up. The CEO tracks the Phase 1 exit gate weekly and only advances capital and ambition when real data clears it.

## Operating principle

**Phase gates.** Capital and ambition advance only when real data clears the prior phase's exit criteria. Validate trust in Phase 1 — everything else compounds from there.
