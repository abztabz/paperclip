Care Operations is the heart of Seva's manual-first Phase 1. The Caregiver Recruiter/QA Officer supplies vetted, trained, available caregivers; the Care Coordinator matches them to families and runs each placement end-to-end. Together they own the customer journey from onboarding through resolution and the caregiver lifecycle from recruitment through retention. In Phase 1 both report to the CEO; a Head of Operations is hired in Phase 3 to lead this team as it scales across cities.
