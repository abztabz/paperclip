## 3. Unit economics & expected return

**Acquisition efficiency**
- Target **300+ waitlist signups** by T-0. Pure variable spend (content 60k + paid 60k = 120k) → at a conservative NPR 480–800 per waitlist signup, yields ~150–250 signups; NRNA + hospital + organic deliver the rest at ~zero marginal cost.
- Blended pre-launch waitlist CAC target: **< NPR 1,500** (vs the plan's paid-social placement CAC of ~NPR 5,000) — pre-commitment signups are far cheaper than paid placements.

**Payback logic (planning assumptions, not guarantees)**
- Say 300 waitlist → **10% convert** to a paid placement in the first ~2 months = **~30 placements** seeded at near-zero incremental CAC.
- Per the plan: avg gross care spend NPR 32,000/placement/mo × 22% Syahar take = ~NPR 7,040/mo; × ~2.5-month avg duration = **~NPR 17,600 net revenue/placement**.
- 30 placements → **~NPR 528,000 net revenue** vs NPR 250,000 spend ≈ **~2.1× direct payback**, before counting (a) the durable video/brand assets, (b) lowered CAC on *all* later placements, and (c) the testimonials that feed every future channel.
- Break-even on the full NPR 250k needs only **~14 seeded placements** (~5% waitlist conversion). The margin of safety is large.
