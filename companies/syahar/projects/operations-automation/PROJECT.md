CEO-initiated workstream (2026-06-16): reduce operating cost — especially the People/payroll (38% of Y5 opex) and Marketing (23%) lines — by replacing back-office knowledge work with Claude agents, as proven with the marketing pod. Keep humans where the value is physical, relational, or trust-bearing.

> **⭐ Golden Rule still applies:** Syahar is a caregiver platform; automation serves care quality and unit economics, never replaces the human reassurance families pay for.
