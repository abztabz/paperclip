## About Syahar

Syahar is Nepal's first cross-border caregiver placement platform, connecting in-patient families in Nepal with verified, trained caregivers. We serve the Nepali diaspora — families abroad whose elderly parents or recovering relatives need professional in-home care in Kathmandu and beyond.

Our caregivers are background-checked, health-safety trained, and rated by families after every placement. We currently operate in Kathmandu Valley with plans to expand to Pokhara, Biratnagar, and Butwal by end of 2026.

---

## The Opportunity

Over **3 million Nepalis** living abroad send remittances home. A significant portion of those families also carry the burden of arranging care for aging parents or ill relatives — remotely, across time zones, often through informal channels.

**[PARTNER NAME]** reaches these exact families at the moment they are most financially engaged with Nepal. A Syahar integration lets them do more than send money — they can send care.
